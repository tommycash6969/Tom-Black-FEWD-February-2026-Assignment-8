Common Questions

Everything you need to know

1. Do I need a domain name to get started?

Not at all. I can help you secure a domain through a trusted provider, or work with your existing domain if you already have one.

2. Do I need hosting before we begin?

No. I will guide you through the best hosting options for your goals and budget. If you already have hosting, I can work with that too.

3. Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. If your current site feels outdated or underperforms, I can rebuild it into a modern, conversion-focused experience.

4. How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites are completed within 5 to 10 working days, depending on scope and complexity.

5. Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every site is built responsively for mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

6. Can I update the website myself after it is done?

Definitely. I build user-friendly websites and can show you how to manage updates with confidence.

7. Do you offer eCommerce functionality?

Yes. I can set up full WooCommerce stores with products, payments, and order management.

8. Will my website be SEO-friendly?

Yes. I apply on-page SEO best practices to improve visibility and ranking potential.

9. What do you need from me to get started?

Basic business details, any branding assets, and your direction. If content is missing, I can help shape that too.

10. Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Ongoing maintenance and support are available to keep your site stable and up to date.

11. What makes your service different?

I focus on clean design, performance, and clarity so your website is not only attractive but effective for business outcomes.

Still have a question?

Drop me a message and I will get back to you within one business day.
