Reskin Aesthetics Website

Client
Reskin Aesthetics — a skin and beauty clinic based in the Western Cape, South Africa.
Brief
The client needed a premium, trust-focused website that could showcase their treatment menu, build credibility with new patients, and drive enquiry bookings. The existing site was outdated, hard to navigate, and did not reflect the quality of the treatments on offer.
Stack
WordPress, Elementor Builder, Custom Theme Styling, UX Content Architecture, Local SEO
What Was Delivered
  • Full site redesign with a clean, premium visual identity
  • Structured treatment pages with clear service descriptions and pricing tiers
  • Mobile-first responsive layout optimised for smartphone browsing
  • Contact and booking call-to-action placement on every key page
  • Local SEO basics — meta titles, descriptions, and Google Business alignment
Challenge
The previous layout felt cluttered and inconsistent. Users had to hunt for treatment details, and there was no clear path from discovery to enquiry. The brand lacked visual cohesion across pages.
Solution
Rebuilt the full information architecture around treatment intent — grouping services logically, adding visual hierarchy to guide the eye, and placing booking prompts at natural decision points. A consistent colour and typography system was applied across all pages.
Outcome
Measurably cleaner user journey from landing to enquiry. The client reported an increase in direct booking requests within the first month of launch. Staff also noted fewer phone calls asking basic questions — a sign that the site was doing its job.
What I Learned
Healthcare-adjacent clients need trust signals built into the design — credentials, before/after results (where appropriate), and clear contact options. Clarity is the product.

Sunglide Online B2B Store

Client
Sunglide — a wholesale distribution company supplying window and door hardware to trade customers across South Africa.
Brief
Sunglide needed a B2B-focused WooCommerce store that could handle multiple customer types — retail, trade, and wholesale — each with different pricing structures. The goal was to reduce manual quoting and let trade clients order directly online without exposing retail pricing to wholesale accounts.
Stack
WordPress, WooCommerce, Product Variations, Tiered Pricing Rules, User Role Management
What Was Delivered
  • Role-based pricing system — different price tiers visible per account type
  • Clean product catalogue with variation-based stock management
  • Streamlined checkout flow tailored to B2B order quantities
  • Trade account application form and manual approval workflow
  • Mobile-responsive layout that works in warehouse and on-the-go contexts
Challenge
Standard WooCommerce does not natively support tiered B2B pricing in an intuitive way. Displaying the right price to the right user without breaking the shopping experience — or revealing sensitive wholesale rates to retail browsers — required careful architecture decisions.
Solution
Implemented structured user roles tied to account registration, with conditional pricing rules applied per role. Product visibility rules ensured wholesale-only lines were not browsable by unregistered users. The checkout flow was simplified to reduce friction for repeat trade orders.
Outcome
Reduced the volume of manual quote requests by giving trade customers self-serve ordering capability. The client was able to onboard new wholesale accounts faster and with less administrative overhead.
What I Learned
B2B e-commerce requires thinking beyond the cart. Account management, pricing logic, and trust at the wholesale level are just as important as the front-end presentation. Understanding the client's internal processes was essential before writing a single line of code.

BlackStudio Paarl Print-on-Demand Catalogue

Client
BlackStudio Paarl — a creative brand in the Winelands offering custom printed merchandise through a print-on-demand service model.
Brief
BlackStudio needed a WordPress-based product pricing catalogue where customers could browse print-on-demand items, compare options quickly, and submit informed enquiries with clear expectations on pricing and fulfilment windows.
Stack
WordPress, Product Pricing Catalogue Architecture, Print-on-Demand Workflow Content, Responsive Layout, Content Structuring
What Was Delivered
  • Product-first catalogue with clear categories for apparel and branded items
  • Pricing-focused layout to support quick comparison and faster decision-making
  • Structured product pages with size, colour, print option, and pricing clarity
  • Messaging around production windows and delivery expectations
  • Brand-consistent visual system to keep the shop identity strong and recognisable
Challenge
Print-on-demand introduces customer uncertainty around lead times, product variations, and final print output. The site needed to build trust while keeping order steps simple and clear.
Solution
Reworked the information architecture around product discovery and pricing clarity. Product cards were structured for quick scanning, variation options were clarified at product level, and fulfilment messaging was placed at key decision points so customers understood exactly how the print-on-demand process worked before making contact.
Outcome
The business gained a clearer product and pricing reference point for custom merchandise, reducing manual back-and-forth before enquiries. Customers were able to browse options and pricing more confidently, which improved lead quality and reduced repetitive support questions.
What I Learned
For print-on-demand businesses, clarity around expectations is as important as visual design. When customers understand options, timing, and fulfilment upfront, trust and conversion improve naturally.
